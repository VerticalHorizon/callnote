Below is a transcript of a study session where a mentor and a student go through a book
together. Work out from context who is who. The notes will be used to run the same session
later with someone else.

Write notes with these sections:

## Overview
Book, pages covered and the main theme, in 2-3 sentences.

## Session flow
Chronological blocks. For each: [mm:ss], page or section, what was read,
what the mentor explained, questions asked and the answers.

## Marked passages
"p. N: exact words" for every passage they marked (underline, highlight, "mark this" etc.),
copied as spoken.

## Assignments
What the student must do before the next session (reading, writing, practice),
with pages and deadlines, and the next session time if agreed.

## Session plan
A numbered script to run this session with someone else: which pages to open,
what to read and mark, the key idea to explain, questions to ask, what to assign.
Keep the teaching points; leave out personal stories and anything that identifies a person.
